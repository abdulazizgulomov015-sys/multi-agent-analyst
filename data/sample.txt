Coffee is a brewed drink prepared from roasted coffee beans, which are the seeds of berries from certain Coffea species. Coffee is darkly colored, bitter, and slightly acidic, and has a stimulating effect on humans, primarily due to its caffeine content.

Coffee is one of the most popular drinks in the world and can be prepared and presented in a variety of ways. It is usually served hot, although chilled or iced coffee is common. Studies show that moderate coffee consumption is safe for most adults and may even have health benefits.
